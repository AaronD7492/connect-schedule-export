Connect Schedule Export

Put your Amazon Connect schedule in your phone's calendar. The tool runs in your own browser while you're signed in to your Agent Schedule page. It reads your shifts and gives you a calendar (.ics) file. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it never sees your password.

Version {{VERSION}}. Use it on a personal device. Company devices block scripts.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add Bookmark, and save this page as a bookmark.
  3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the new bookmark. Rename it Export schedule, clear the address and paste the code. Tap Done.
  4. Open your Agent Schedule page in Amazon Connect, open Bookmarks and tap Export schedule.
  5. Tap Download .ics, open Safari's downloads list, tap the file, then Add All.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap
  2. Tap the star to bookmark this page, then tap Edit. Name it Export schedule and paste the code over the URL.
  3. On your Agent Schedule page, tap the address bar, type Export schedule and tap the bookmark in the suggestions (it shows a star).
  4. Tap Download .ics, open the file from your notifications or Downloads and confirm the import in Google Calendar. If your phone can't open it, import it at calendar.google.com on a computer.

Computer

Chrome or Firefox: show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag this button onto it:

Export schedule

Edge, or if dragging doesn't work:

  1. Click
  2. Press Ctrl+D to bookmark this page. Name it Export schedule and save it to the favorites bar.
  3. Right-click the new favorite, choose Edit, and replace the URL with the copied code.

Then open your Agent Schedule page and click the bookmark. Open the downloaded file with Outlook or Apple Calendar, or import it in Google Calendar under Settings → Import & export.

Diagnostics

If the tool doesn't work, install this second bookmark the same way and run it on your schedule page. It checks the page, moves one week forward and back, and shows results you can copy and send to the developer.
